Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cozmic collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you access or use our website, create an account, manage your profile, publish content, communicate with other users, or participate in groups.

Please read this policy carefully before using Cozmic. By using the platform, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular activity, we will seek that consent separately.

Important privacy notice: Cozmic profiles, posts, and videos are intended to be publicly visible by default under the current platform settings. Do not publish information that you do not want other people to see.

1. Who We Are

Cozmic is a social platform designed to let users create profiles, connect with friends, share posts and videos, and communicate with other people.

For this policy, "Cozmic", "we", "our", and "us" refer to the person or organisation operating the platform.

The operator's full legal name and a reliable privacy contact address should be published here before the platform is launched publicly.

2. Information We May Collect

The information processed by Cozmic depends on the features you use and the information you choose to provide.

A. Account Information

This may include:

  • Your account identifier.
  • Your email address, where collected for registration or authentication.
  • Your username.
  • Your display name or full name.
  • Authentication and account-status information.
  • Account creation and related timestamps.

Authentication information may be processed by the service used to manage your login. We do not intend to request or display your account password in your public profile.

B. Profile Information

Depending on the fields available, your profile may contain a profile photograph, biography, location information, or other details you choose to add.

Only provide location or personal details you are comfortable sharing with the people who can access your profile.

C. Content You Publish

We may process the content you upload or publish, including:

  • Text posts and captions.
  • Photographs and other images.
  • Videos and video descriptions.
  • Comments and reactions, where available.
  • Group information and content you submit to groups.
  • Information associated with content, such as its publication time.

D. Communications

If you use messaging or group-chat features, the platform may process message content, sender and recipient identifiers, timestamps, group membership, and related information needed to deliver and display conversations.

The exact information stored depends on the features implemented in the application.

E. Friendships and Group Membership

We may process information about friend connections, friend requests, group memberships, and your interactions with other users.

F. Technical and Usage Information

Depending on the hosting environment, browser, security configuration, and services in use, technical information may include:

  • Browser type and version.
  • Device or operating-system information.
  • IP addresses recorded by infrastructure providers.
  • Security and diagnostic logs.
  • Basic information about errors or service requests.

We do not claim to collect every item listed above. The information actually collected depends on the services and logging configurations in operation.

3. How Information Is Collected

Information may be collected when you:

  • Create or sign in to an account.
  • Complete or update your profile.
  • Upload a photograph, video, or other content.
  • Send messages or participate in a group.
  • Make or respond to friend requests.
  • Submit an account-deletion request.
  • Contact the platform operator for assistance.
  • Use the website, which may generate technical logs through its hosting services.

Some information is provided directly by you. Other information may be generated as part of operating the platform or supplied by the technical providers that support it.

4. How We Use Information

Subject to applicable law, information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Authenticating users and protecting account access.
  • Displaying profiles and published content.
  • Delivering posts, photographs, and videos.
  • Supporting friendships and group membership.
  • Delivering messages and displaying conversations.
  • Processing account deactivation and deletion requests.
  • Responding to support enquiries.
  • Detecting misuse, spam, security problems, and fraud.
  • Maintaining and troubleshooting the platform.
  • Meeting legal obligations and responding to lawful requests.

We should only process personal information where we have an appropriate legal basis, such as performing our service agreement with you, complying with legal obligations, protecting legitimate interests, or obtaining consent where required.

We will not treat this list as permission to use information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis.

5. Public Profiles and Content

Under Cozmic's current intended settings, profiles, posts, and videos are public by default.

This means that people who can access the relevant pages may be able to view or interact with this information without being your friend.

Depending on how the platform is configured, public information may include:

  • Your username and display name.
  • Your profile photograph and biography.
  • Posts, captions, photographs, and videos you publish.
  • Comments or reactions you make visible to others.
  • Publicly displayed group or friendship information.

The exact information visible to other users depends on the pages, features, and access controls that are actually implemented.

Think Before You Post

Do not publish passwords, bank details, identity documents, private addresses, or other sensitive information in a public profile, post, caption, photograph, or video.

Other users may take screenshots, download media, copy information, or share public content outside Cozmic. We cannot guarantee that those copies can be located or removed.

Deleting an original post may not remove copies previously saved or redistributed by other people.

6. Messages, Friends, and Groups

Cozmic may process information needed to support direct messages, group conversations, friend connections, and group membership.

Messages may be visible to the people participating in the conversation. Group messages may be visible to members who can access that group or its message history.

Depending on the implementation, message records may remain in storage after a participant signs out or deactivates an account.

Do not assume that messages are end-to-end encrypted unless Cozmic explicitly confirms that the particular messaging feature provides this protection.

We do not promise that every conversation is confidential against all forms of access. Access controls, technical service providers, applicable law, and the actual implementation determine how communications are handled.

7. Storage and Service Providers

Cozmic uses Supabase technology for backend functionality, including database and authentication features. Depending on the configuration, Supabase services may also be used for file or media storage.

Your information may therefore be stored or processed by the service providers supporting the platform. Their role and the processing that occurs depend on the services configured by the operator.

These providers may process information to provide hosting, database operations, authentication, storage, security, and related technical services.

We should configure these services with appropriate access controls, database permissions, storage permissions, and security measures.

Information about the provider's own practices can be found in the Supabase Privacy Policy .

This link is provided for reference. It does not replace our responsibility to explain how Cozmic itself handles information.

8. When Information May Be Shared

We do not intend to sell users' personal information as a business practice. Information may be disclosed in the following circumstances, subject to applicable law.

A. With Other Users

Information you publish publicly may be visible to other users and, depending on access and sharing, people outside Cozmic.

B. With Service Providers

Technical providers may process information when needed to operate the platform, including database hosting, authentication, media storage, and website infrastructure.

C. For Legal Reasons

We may disclose information when required by law, a valid legal process, or a lawful request from a competent authority.

D. To Protect People and the Platform

Where legally permitted, we may disclose or preserve relevant information when reasonably necessary to investigate suspected fraud, serious security incidents, unlawful activity, or threats to safety.

E. Business Changes

If the operation of Cozmic is transferred, reorganised, or otherwise changes hands, information may be transferred where legally permitted and with appropriate safeguards.

We will not claim that information is shared only with the categories above if additional recipients or services are actually used. The policy should be updated when the platform's data-sharing arrangements change.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website and its technical providers may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies to support login sessions, security, preferences, and essential website functions.

The exact technologies used depend on the current implementation and the services enabled.

If analytics, advertising, or other non-essential tracking technologies are introduced, this policy should be updated to describe them and any consent choices required by applicable law.

Blocking cookies or browser storage may affect login or other website features that rely on them.

10. Data Security

We recognise the importance of protecting personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Appropriate safeguards may include:

  • Authentication and account-access controls.
  • Database access policies and permissions.
  • Restricted access to administrative functions.
  • Secure connections supported by the hosting environment.
  • Appropriate protection of uploaded files and media.
  • Monitoring and investigation of suspected security problems.

These are safeguards that should be implemented and maintained, not a guarantee that every measure is currently active or that a breach can never occur.

No website, storage system, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will take appropriate steps as required by applicable law, which may include investigating the incident, mitigating harm, and notifying affected people or relevant authorities where legally required.

11. How Long Information Is Kept

We aim to keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods may depend on:

  • Whether your account remains active.
  • Whether the information is needed to provide a feature.
  • Security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution needs.
  • Legal obligations to preserve certain records.
  • Technical backup and recovery arrangements.

We do not promise that every category of information has a fixed deletion period unless that period has been established and can be followed in practice.

We should periodically review stored information and remove or anonymise it when there is no longer a lawful and necessary reason to retain it.

12. Account Deactivation

Cozmic may allow you to temporarily deactivate your account through the account interface.

Deactivation is not the same as permanent deletion. It may change your account status or sign you out while some associated information remains stored.

Depending on the platform's current implementation, deactivation may not automatically remove your existing posts, videos, messages, group activity, or other records from every part of the service.

We cannot promise that deactivation hides all information unless the platform's access controls have been configured to enforce that result.

If you want your account information considered for permanent deletion, follow the deletion request process described below.

13. Permanent Account Deletion

Cozmic provides a process for requesting permanent account deletion.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Sign in to your Cozmic account.
  2. Open your profile or account settings.
  3. Select the permanent account deletion option, if available.
  4. Submit the request through the provided interface.
  5. Follow any additional instructions supplied by the platform operator.

How Requests Are Processed

Under the current process, submitting a deletion request does not automatically delete your account. Requests are recorded for review, and an administrator must manually process approved requests.

A request marked as pending has not necessarily been approved or completed. Processing times may vary, and we should handle valid requests within the time required by applicable law.

What May Happen After Deletion

Once an approved request is processed, the operator should take the appropriate steps to delete the account and associated personal information that is no longer needed or lawfully required to be kept.

Depending on the database relationships and deletion procedures, information associated with your account may include profile records, posts, photographs, videos, comments, friendships, group memberships, or messages.

We do not promise that all these categories are automatically deleted together. Their removal depends on the platform's actual database design, storage configuration, and deletion procedure.

Some information may need to be retained for legal compliance, security, dispute resolution, or other lawful purposes. Information in backups may also persist until the relevant backup is overwritten or expires under the applicable retention process.

Content already copied, screenshotted, downloaded, or shared by other users may remain outside Cozmic's control.

If you need to know the status of your request, contact the platform operator through the official contact channel once one has been published.

14. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally available.
  • Object to or request restrictions on certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request information about how your personal data is handled.
  • Complain to the appropriate data protection authority.

These rights are subject to the conditions, exceptions, and procedures established by applicable law. Some information may need to be retained where a lawful exception applies.

How to Exercise Your Rights

Use the account features available to you or contact the platform operator through the official privacy contact channel.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, so that another person cannot improperly access or delete your information.

We aim to respond to valid requests in accordance with applicable legal deadlines.

15. Children and Age Requirements

Cozmic is intended for people aged 13 and older, subject to any higher minimum age or parental authorisation required by applicable law.

We do not knowingly intend to collect personal information from children who are not legally permitted to use the service.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable age requirements, contact the platform operator through the official contact channel so the situation can be reviewed.

If we determine that information was collected unlawfully, we will take appropriate steps as required by applicable law.

16. International Data Transfers

Cozmic's technical service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live.

The locations involved depend on the providers, project settings, and infrastructure used to operate the platform.

Where personal information is transferred across borders, we will take the steps required by applicable law to establish an appropriate legal basis and any required safeguards.

The operator should confirm the actual hosting and storage locations and update this section if the arrangements change.

17. Third-Party Websites and Services

Cozmic may contain links to websites, media, or services operated by third parties.

If you follow a third-party link, that service may collect information under its own privacy policy and terms.

We do not control the privacy practices of independent third-party websites. Review their policies before providing personal information.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Where required by law, we will provide notice of material changes or seek consent before introducing processing that requires it.
